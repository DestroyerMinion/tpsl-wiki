Zeylon is the main pilot of the pirate crew, with several decades of piracy under his belt. He has sailed several well known ships, and has been convinced to come out of retirement by [[Blaze]].
Zeylon has chestnut coloured skin and greying hair that he keeps in box braids pulled into a ponytail with the sides of his head shaved clean. All the years of sailing on the seas have kept him lean and muscular, although the retirement years have given him a bit of a barrel rather than a six pack. He is a bit gruff, but a real teddy bear once you get to know him.
