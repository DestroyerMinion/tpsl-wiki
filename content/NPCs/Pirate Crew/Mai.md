Nenita is a fairly introverted and shy woman of Japanese decent who tends to keep to herself unless her duties demand it. She is a skilled markswoman and assists [[Master Gunner]] in the handling and preparation of the firearms. Standing around 163 tall, curvy, she is a sort of wall flower and needs some encouragement to open up and engage. Her family taught her archery, but she dismissed it in this new age of gunpowder and left to study marksmanship, finding great teachers in the gunners she met on the crews on her journeys.
