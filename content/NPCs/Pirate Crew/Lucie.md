Lucie has been helping out in the Proud Marys kitchen for a while now, mainly helping out with mise-en-place and specialized dishes due to her familiarity and skill with knives and really anything sharp. Her smaller stature of a round 155 initially made it hard for her to be taken seriously by some of the patrons, but a few biting retorts (and in one case an actual bite) cleared those misunderstandings right up. She has pale skin from her recent years of staying inside, long black hair that reaches down to her hips and keeps meticulously braided, and one black and one pale blue eye. This from an experiment to due trying to dye her eye black went a bit wrong, almost costing her her sight.

She is very direct and enthusiastic, speaking her mind, but can be misconstrued as insensitive or ill intentioned. She also is going to function as the ships surgeon, a "hobby" she picked up as due to her interest in knives and anything sharp.
