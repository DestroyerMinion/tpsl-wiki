The Fury is a fairly mysterious Pirate Queen in charge of a fleet of roughly 20 or so ships at a time, and was previously mainly seen to the south, in the Caribbean. In recent weeks, her name and reputation have spread farther North to the Bahamas, as she seems to have set her sight on new hunting grounds after [[The Spanish Town Slaughter]]. Prior to this raid, many in the Bahamas may have heard her name, but did not think much further on it, that was the Caribbeans problem.

Now however, tongues are wagging and the flames of fear are fanned, The Fury is coming to the Bahamas. There have not been many sightings of the fleet itself, as it tends to stay away from larger towns, and most are to terrified to approach closely enough to track their progress, but it is undeniable it is north of Cuba and out of the Caribbean.

The motivation for this sudden change in preferred prowling grounds is unknown outside the fleet, but it is curious that it occurred so shortly after the raid of Spanish Town. The most popular theories being that she is running from the English, or looking to gather even more pirates under her flag to mount a bigger resistance to their retaliation. Whatever the motivation may be, the events at Spanish town have not endeared her to Bahamian villages and towns, or other pirates for that matter. Many of them now fearing the retaliation of the English could be aimed at all pirates, not just They Fury.
