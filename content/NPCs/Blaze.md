The owner and main bartender of [Nassaus](Nassau) cleanest, and dirtiest, drinking establishment, the Proud Mary, Blaze can usually be found pouring drinks or observing the crowd. The inn has garnered a reputation of acceptance and openness, only discriminating against the assholes and troublemakers who are likely visiting Nassau for the very first time, and do not realize they are in over their heads. Depending on their reputation and recent fortunes, Blaze will try to calm the situation and give them a chance to make amends. Should that fail, they are catapulted inelegantly in a tangled pile of limbs into the streets by the inns enforcer/bouncer [[Grace]].

Blaze herself is in her 50s, with a shock of wavy red hair, that has in recent years become interwoven with the occasional strand and root of grey. Her pale green eyes sweep over the crowds gathering at her inn as she walks from table to table, making small talk, learning of recent events and taking orders. The atmosphere is relaxed, although you can tell the some people become nervous as she approaches their table flashing them a grin and striking up a conversation.

Having been single as long as she has been in Nassau, Blaze is not shy about taking a patron for a tumble between the sheets for some stress relief. But such cases do not happen too frequently, as she seems quite selective about her company, and these hookups never progressed beyond a shared desire for a pleasurable night, as visitors to Nassau tend not to stick around too long.

"Long-term" clientele of [[Proud Mary's]] have noticed a recent new addition to the inns staff: a large German Shepard that dutifully follows Blaze on her rounds, furnished with small pouches to hold a variety of things Blaze may need. When the inn becomes too crowded he retires up the stairs to a position by the railing, it's cool blue eyes calmly assessing the patrons, ready to assist should Blaze call for him.
