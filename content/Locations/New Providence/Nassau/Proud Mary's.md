Proud Marys may be one of many taverns in Nassau, but it is by far the most reputable one, run by Blaze, flirtatious and welcoming host, and the unofficial Pirate Lord of Nassau. The inn offers the usual services you find in other establishments, a warm place to sleep, a drink, and decent food, but that is not why patrons frequent the inn.

Compared to other inns in the pirate town, it is a safe place to rest your weary head without fear of judgement or prejudice, all are welcome that abide by the taverns essentially one single rule: Don't be an ass and cause us or other patrons any trouble. It is as simple as that, or at least it should be. See someone you don't like ignore them, or take it to the streets or the sheets to either fight and/or fuck it out (consensually of course).

For the later case Proud Marys does rent out rooms by the hour for anyone needing a space or certain... equipment for their hook-ups. These rooms are furnished in a variety of styles to suit different needs, some of them including larger beds or bath tubs should they be required. Proud Marys itself does not hire or employ sex workers, but does provide them with a safe place to conduct business on their terms and protection in the rare case it is required.

The tavern stretches a grand three stories tall, with each floor meeting different needs, and having been given a different nickname based on a sin by the locals. Floor one that serves food and drink and music is nicknamed Gluttony; Floor two caters to your sexual desires and pleasures and is called Lust; And finally floor 3 is Sloth, where most wind up after working their way through the first two floors. Mind you these nicknames are not _officially_ accepted by the inn, but you will find the staff slipping up and using them more often than not.

Lastly the tavern houses on larger meeting room in the back used to make deals, work out disputes, or hold the meetings of the Pirate Council of Nassau.
