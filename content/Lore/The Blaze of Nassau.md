The pirate port of [[Nassau]] is by far the largest town on the island of [[New Providence]], and has in the past been the home berth of several notable pirates. It lies on the Northern shore of the island and boasts one of the largest harbours in the Bahamas, allowing up to 500 ships to dock simultaneously, while also being shallow enough to prevent larger battleships from reaching the shore.

Several people have in the past sought to lay claim to the title of Governor of New Providence, but they have always been careful to avoid extending that jurisdiction to Nassau. It has no official governing body, but a council that meets regularly and consists of the captains of the largest crews currently in harbour. This council enforces some basic rules and collects on berthing fees and trading fees that are reinvested to improve the town and its defences.

With active pirate crews always coming and going, the members of the council change frequently, but one exception to the pirate captain rule ahs always been made, for [[Blaze]]. No one on the current council can quite explain why she is there, but at the same time does not question the decision, she has more than earned her place. While the rest of the council might disappear on raids, Blaze always stays behind keeps the books straight and ensure Nassau runs smoothly behind the scenes.

How she got on the council to begin with no one has an answer to. The game of telephone goes back several generations of the council (7-8 years), each new council members saying the previous council vouched for her. And rightly so. Without her steadying presence Nassau would have succumbed to the chaos other pirate havens quickly fall to. Her involvement is not spoken of loudly, but the rumours are persistent enough that it has become an accepted fact: Blaze is the de facto leader and Lord of the pirates of Nassau.

Her past is a mystery, since the inhabitants of Nassau come and go, but some of the old timers say she arrived just over 10 years ago. Starting of inconspicuous running her tavern, she soon saw more and more customers and got dragged into resolving their disputes, often being sought out as a neutral third party to resolve larger feuds. Tales about her past are many and colourful, ascribing both great infamy and heritage to her in equal measure, but they all conclude with a similar warning: if you decide to dock in Nassau, follow the rules and pay your dues, or you will be scorched by the Blaze.
