Cannon fire, raucous singing, and screams of both pleasure and pain are no strangers to the pirate haven of New Providence. The Era of Piracy is in full swing and New Providence is alive with those seeking their fortunes and adventure (of one kind or another). Sooner or later most of these thirsty lasses find their way to Proud Mary's, Nassau's foremost den of drinking and degeneracy.

Blaze, the bars owner, is in her fifties with a tangled mess of fiery red curls that are starting to be threaded with white, and welcomes anyone and everyone as long as they don't cause (unwanted) trouble. For years Mary's has been a hub for those seeking a crew or those looking to join one, and until recently Blaze has just been a facilitator, but that is all changing...

2 nights ago Blaze posted her own flyer in Mary's, looking to recruit a crew to help her track down The Fury, a Pirate Queen that recently shifted her attention from the Caribbean to the Bahamas. Blaze is seeking retribution, The Fury having robbed her of the love of her life years ago. The bar is more crowded than usual as you arrive on this last day of recruitment, hoping to make the cut for the crew, and looking forward to the promised debauchery that is to be Proud Mary's closing party before the crew sets out to sea!

Sing, drink, and party the night away! One last chance for a casual encounter or the start of something greater, as from tomorrow morning on you will be confined to much more intimate quarters for the foreseeable future...
