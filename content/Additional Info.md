## Length

The mini-campaign is planned to take around 6-8 sessions, depending a bit on how sidetracked we get along the way. (Already taken this into account and expanded the length beyond what I initially planned :P)

## Tone

The intention is for this to be a more light-hearted and flirty adventure with some serious moment here and there. As mentioned previously, Pirates of the Caribbean is a good comparison. As everyone surely expects, it will be a fairly flirtatious campaign with as much of a mix of characters as possible, all from shy wall-flowers to straight forward playgirls/boys. The intention is to play out the flirting and potentially some of the caressing/making out if people are comfortable with it, but fading to black for the actual action.

Players can at any point ask to fade to black no questions asked. If anyone has anything they would like me to know in advance that they would like to avoid (or include), just send me a message and I will be sure to take it into account <3

(As per usual, I will not including anything SA related, as I do not wish to describe anything of the sort and I want this to be more light-hearted)

## Setting

The setting takes place in a fictionalised version of the Golden Era of Piracy of the early 1700's. Tortuga has been replaced by the port of Nassau on the island of New Providence as one of the main Pirate Havens of the area, offering shelter and trade to the free spirited raiders. It does have a code that it's inhabitants and visitors are expected to follow, and those who break the code are banned from entering the port of Nassau.

Being inspired by Pirates of the Caribbean, magic/supernatural elements are present, and I will not be limiting race/ethnicity creativity as long as it fits the setting. This means if you wish to create a character that is related to the Kraken somehow, please do (no judgement here ;)), but I would ask that they can present as human for a set period of time at least (some ritual/action they have to perform periodically to retain their human form f.ex.), as the supernatural/magic is not common place, and it will make it a bit smoother to interact with the rest of the world.

## Gender Distribution

As we are playing Thirsty Sword Lesbians, the intention is to do a bit of a reversal of the usual distribution of men and women, meaning that a majority of the "main" characters you will encounter will be women/trans/non-binary. That is not to say there will be no men of course, but they will for the most part have minor roles. I feel this fits the intended and expected setting best given the system we are using, but am open to counter arguments should anyone have them of course.

As players you are of course free to create your character of any gender, sexuality, and with any pronouns you wish.

## Hook/Motivation

As mentioned in the teaser, the start of the journey will be you trying to convince Blaze to join her crew on her adventure to find The Fury. The motivation for this I leave up to you. Maybe you have a grudge against The Fury yourself, you are looking to prove yourself against on of the foremost Pirate Queens of the era, you have an connection to another crew member and are joining to keep them safe, or maybe you are just looking for some adventure and fun (I have several other examples but don't wish to mention them in the group chat to avoid placing ideas in anyone's head or ruin anyone's plan by mentioning what they have already considered).

If anyone wants to already start planning out some possibilities, just shoot me a message and we can start to plan!

## Love Interests and Hook Ups

Given the system we are playing, like I mentioned earlier, it is absolutely planed for your characters to get some one-on-one (or one-on-one-on-one-on-tentacle) time with NPCs (or PCs) should you and they wish it. It would be helpful for me to know a bit more about what makes your character tick in that regard, what are their likes/dislikes in a partner? That way I can plan around it a bit and try to include someone or multiple people that your characters can fool around with ;)
